Enterprise lending technology

The system of record for the institutions that issue credit.

LoanApp runs a lending operation end to end: the campaigns you take to market and who they are aimed at, origination and underwriting, executed agreements, the repayment instruments banks and employers act on, and the analysis that tells you how the book is performing. Every institution on it operates as its own tenant — its own people, its own portfolio, its own data.
