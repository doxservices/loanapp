How a tenant is put together

Its own address

A lender lives at its own path — /loanit-financing — and that is where its customers apply, follow a loan and open a ticket.

Its own people

Business admins, loan officers, underwriters and support staff belong to one business, and see only that business's customers and records.

Permissions, not pages

What a person can open is decided by the permissions on their role, and the menu is built from those permissions before the page is drawn.

Its own records

Campaigns, applications, contracts, standing orders, salary deductions and tickets are all held against the business that created them.
