What runs on it

One system from the first enquiry to the last repayment.

Campaigns and cohorts Published, unpublished or kept to staff, and offered only to the people who qualify.
Applications Submitted by the customer, reviewed by an underwriter, decided by a loan officer.
Contracts A print-ready agreement, filled from the record and shared on a link that gives nothing away.
Standing orders and salary deductions The repayment authority the bank or the employer needs, with its full schedule.
Support tickets A customer's question, held against the record it is about.
Repayments and statements What is owed, what has been paid, and every payment on a loan.

How a tenant is put together

Its own address
