The repayment schedule in this agreement will remain in effect and enforceable unless the loan is repaid in full or otherwise discharged under applicable law. Any extension of time, adjustment of payments, or waiver of any term by the lender does not change the borrower’s obligation to make payments according to this schedule unless agreed to in writing by both parties.

  1. The borrower agrees to make each repayment in the amount and on the date specified in the Repayment Schedule. Payments must be made in full and in a manner verifiable by the lender, on or before the scheduled due date.
  2. Grace Period: The borrower is allowed a grace period of three (3) days after the scheduled due date to make payment without penalty.
  3. If payment is not received in full by the end of the grace period:
    • The instalment will be considered overdue.
    • A late payment fee of J$2,000 per overdue instalment will be added to the outstanding loan balance.
    • Interest will continue to accrue daily on the outstanding balance at the rate stated in the Loan Details until payment is made in full.
  4. The borrower must not take any action intended to avoid or delay repayment, including but not limited to:
    • Removing or cancelling any standing order or automatic payment arrangement;
    • Changing the primary salary account used for repayment without providing at least seven (7) days’ prior written notice to the lender.
  5. Any such action that results in the loan becoming overdue will be treated as a breach of this agreement.
Borrower’s Rights

You may repay the loan in full before the maturity date without penalty. You have the right to lodge a complaint with Loan It Limited at [complaints email/phone], and if unresolved, escalate to the Consumer Affairs Commission or BOJ.

Default & Recovery

If the borrower fails to make a scheduled payment on a loan already classified as non-performing, and such failure continues for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, the loan will be deemed in default. Upon default, the lender may, after giving written notice to the borrower, demand immediate payment of the total outstanding balance of the loan, including any accrued interest and late fees, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

If the borrower has not paid the total outstanding loan balance, including late fees, within thirty (30) days after default, a penalty of 30% will be applied to the outstanding amount.

The lender may engage a licensed third-party debt collector or attorney to recover the debt. The borrower will be responsible for all costs of recovery, including legal fees, court costs, and collection expenses, as permitted by law.

Data Privacy

Your personal data will be kept secure and only shared with third parties as required by law or for loan recovery purposes.

Seizure Clause

In the event of any breach of this loan agreement by the Borrower, the Lender shall have the absolute right, without further notice, to seize and take possession of any asset(s) owned by the Borrower that have a total value up to three times (3x) the outstanding amount of the loan, including applicable collector, bailiff, legal, and court fees. The Borrower expressly agrees that all reasonable expenses incurred by the Lender in the process of asset seizure—including, but not limited to, collector fees, bailiff fees, attorney fees, and court costs—shall be added to the amount recoverable. The Lender’s rights under this clause are in addition to any other remedies available under law or this agreement.
